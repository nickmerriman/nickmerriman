Nick M. Merriman

PhD Fellow in Forest Modelling
University of Copenhagen, Department of Geosciences and Natural Resource Management

PhD fellow in forest modelling with professional experience in Canadian forestry and wildfire suppression. My research focuses on modelling tree and stand development and allometry using both parametric and machine learning approaches with large scale national forest inventory and experimental datasets.
